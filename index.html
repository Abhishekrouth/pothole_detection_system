About Inspector

This system logs and tracks road safety hazards in real-time.

Using your phone's camera, it leverages a trained YOLO computer vision model to detect potholes. The application automatically links every detection to your exact live GPS coordinates, compiling a downloadable CSV report ready to share with highway authorities.
